# Tinker & Bolt: how to play

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox (phone or desktop). No install, no network needed.
Your progress, settings and the level you are in are saved in the browser automatically.

## The story
Help Bolt, a little clay robot, reopen Mr. Tinker's toy-repair shop. Win levels to earn stars, then spend stars on
**Restore** to fix the shop step by step. Chapter 1 (match-3) fixes the storefront; chapter 2 (screws) repairs the toys.

## Chapter 1: match-3
- Drag a candy onto a neighbour (or tap one, then the other) to swap them. The swap must line up 3 or more.
- 4 in a row → rocket (clears a line) · L or T shape → bomb (3×3) · 5 in a row → rainbow ball (clears a colour).
- Goals are shown at the top; finish them before you run out of moves. Leftover moves add bonus points.
- Dust (clear the candy on top), crates (match next to them), twine (match the tied candy), gears (bring them down).

## Chapter 2: screws
- Tap a screw to take it out. It flies into the toolbox of its colour, or waits in the tray.
- A screw under another plate can't be reached. A plate falls when its last screw is out.
- If the tray is full and nothing can move, Bolt is stuck. Watch the "Next" boxes and plan ahead!
- Padlocks open with the gold key screw · "?" screws show their colour once uncovered · tied plates wait for their partner.

## Lives, rewards, hard levels
- 5 lives; losing or quitting a level costs one; one comes back every 20 minutes. Out of lives: wait, or refill for 60 coins.
- Daily login reward (7-day cycle): minutes of **unlimited lives** plus coins or a booster.
- Win 3 levels in a row for a gift box, and every 5 in a row for a big one. A loss resets the streak.
- Every 5th level is **Hard**: no boosters, tighter limits, double coins.
- Boosters: Hammer (smash a candy / pull any screw), Shuffle (chapter 1), Extra slot (+1 tray slot, chapter 2).
- Replay any level from **Levels** to earn more stars.

## Settings
Sound effects (on/off, volume) and vibration (on/off, strong) are separate. There is no music. "Reduce effects" removes
flashes and shaking. "3D shop view" can be turned off (2D picture). **Play stats → Copy report** copies a summary you can
paste to the developer.
