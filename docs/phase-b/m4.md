## 2. The rule as implemented

**Signal** (`m4_signal`), after the close, on unrounded closes up to the run date:
- the S&P 500 (^GSPC) closes at least 15% below its 252-session high, i.e. the highest close of the last 252
  sessions **with today's included** (`rolling(252).max()` in `research/code/14-short-options/s05_crash_and_spike_trades.py`,
  `21-duration/o2_duration.py`, `23-duration-verify/modules23.py`);
- the VIX close (`checks["vix"]`) is 30 or higher. Both thresholds are inclusive.
- **First day only, 90-day cool-down.** The research's chain: a condition day is a signal when
  `(day − last signal).days > 90`. The chain is rebuilt from the whole ^GSPC/VIX history every evening, so a missed
  run never turns a later day of the same crash into a "first day"; `modules.M4.cooldown_until` (= signal + 90 days)
  is a second guard. The cool-down starts at every signal on the primary data, whether or not it is confirmed,
  admitted or filled: a skipped signal is skipped, not deferred.
- Fail closed: no close on the date, fewer than 252 closes, no VIX close today, or a VIX history that does not cover
  the 252-session window (e.g. a CBOE outage where the pipeline patched in only today's Yahoo value).

**Confirmation** (as W10's): `verify_close` on ^GSPC, and the rule re-run on the second source's close; plus
`checks["vix_ok"]`. An unconfirmed signal is an alert (`data`) and a `shadow` ledger record; no trade, no twin entry.

**Then:** one spread at a time (a signal while M4 is open is logged in the shadow ledger), `run.budget_ok()`.

**Structure** (from tonight's chain, `provider.option_chain(root)`, fetched once per run):
- the verified reference close sets the strikes: the confirmed S&P close × 0.1 for XSP; SPY's close only when the
  pipeline's two-source check passed. The chain must be dated today and its spot within 1% of that close, else no
  trade (no SPY fallback on bad data);
- expiry: the latest listed expiry on or before entry + 90 calendar days, entry = the next session, and at least 40
  days away;
- strikes: the quoted call nearest the close (ties go lower) and the quoted call nearest 105% of it; each within 1%
  of its target, else no trade;
- liquidity (design §4, R8): XSP first; **SPY only when XSP fails the liquidity check**.

**Prices** (`fillmodel.order_prices` on tonight's combo quote), rounded to cents: limit = mid + 0.3 × natural width,
stated maximum = mid + 0.5 × natural width.

**Size:** per contract = stated maximum × 100 (the most a contract can cost). Contracts = the nearest whole number
(halves up) to 2% of NAV, cut to fit the 3% per-trade premium cap. Under one contract: skipped (note and ledger).

**Admission:** `risk.admit_premium("M4", root, premium, "us_equity", book, ..., exempt_governor=True,
min_premium_usd=one contract)`, where the book counts lots, open spreads and **every pending buy** (a W10 order
queued tonight takes its room first: first come, first served). Contracts = min(size, floor(allowed / per contract));
under one contract: skipped.

**NEW_TRADE:** `Recommendation("NEW_TRADE", "M4", "T-<date>-M4", ...)` with one `spread_limit` buy intent (taxable
account, position legs long/short, ratio 1), `run.emit`, then `run.count_trade()`.

**Exit** (`m4_exit_check`): the close is planned for the session two trading days before expiry (`exit_date`); the
session before expiry (`last_close_date`) is left for one retry.
- The EXIT email (`reason` "expiry_rule") goes out the evening before the planned close, with fresh prices from
  tonight's chain: limit = mid − 0.3 × natural width, stated minimum = max(0, mid − 0.5 × natural width).
- A close the 10:17 job cancels puts the trade back to "open" (alert); that evening a fresh EXIT goes out for the
  last session (attempt 2, with `retry_of`).
- Once the next session is the expiry itself, no order is sent: an alert says the paper broker's safety net settles
  it at expiry. A settlement fill (intent `None` accepted) closes the record with reason "expiry_settlement"; if no
  fill reaches the hook, `_reconcile_expired` closes the record at intrinsic value once `broker.spreads()` no longer
  holds it.
- No stop and no profit target. Track 14's optional take-profit at 80% of the width was **not adopted**: the design
  lists neither.

**Fills** (`on_spread_fill` / `on_spread_cancel`, from the 10:17 job):
- buy fill → status "open", `fill_date`, `entry_price`, `contracts`, `cost`;
- sell fill → history row {entry/exit dates and prices, `return` = exit / entry − 1, `pnl` (the broker's
  `realized_pnl` when given), `exit_reason`, `profit` = exit > entry}, forecasts resolved;
- cancelled entry → skipped (`modules.M4.skipped`), its forecast voided (a `correction` ledger record), alert; the
  cool-down stands;
- cancelled close → see Exit.

## 3. The 60-DTE twin (`shadow.M4_TWIN`, no emails)

- Same confirmed signal, whatever M4 itself does (open, skipped, not admitted, not filled).
- Pending entry recorded that evening with M4's reference close; entered at the next session's 10:17 ET snapshot at
  the model price mid + 0.3 × natural width, on the listed expiry nearest to, but not beyond, the entry date + 60
  days (≥ 40 days); strikes as M4's.
- Exited at the 10:17 snapshot of the session two trading days before expiry at mid − 0.3 × natural width (retried
  the next session if unpriced); a twin still open on its expiry settles at intrinsic value from the official close
  in the evening run ("expiry_settlement").
- No usable snapshot within two sessions of the entry session: `entry_missed` (logged, dropped).
- Records: `open_trade` and `trades` [{..., "signal_date", "entry_date", "entry_price", "exit_date", "exit_price",
  "return", "exit_reason"}], the keys the monthly shadow table reads. Errors are guarded (alert `shadow`).

## 4. Interfaces

**Hooks** (contract §7): `daily(run, checks)`, `on_spread_fill(run, fill, intent)`, `on_spread_cancel(run, intent,
reason)`, `roots_needed(run) -> set[str]`, `options_job(run, chains)`.
- `roots_needed`: M4's root while it has a trade; XSP and SPY while M4 is flat and "armed" (last close ≥ 10% below
  its high, `probe_drawdown`); the twin's root while its entry is pending or its exit is due.
- `options_job`: the liquidity probe (armed, flat) and the twin.

**Liquidity probe** (design §4: liquidity is "measured from the market-hours snapshot"): while armed, the 10:17 job
runs the liquidity check on the spread a signal that evening would buy (strikes from the 10:17 spot), per root, into
`modules.M4.liquidity_probe` {"date", "results": {root: {"ok", "reasons", "round_trip_frac", ...}}}. That evening,
a root with a result from today uses it; a root without one is checked on tonight's chain. Prices always come from
tonight's chain.

**State** (contract §6; `setdefault` for older states): `modules.M4` = {"open_trade", "history", "cooldown_until"}
plus "last_signal", "armed", "liquidity_probe", "skipped". `open_trade`: {"trade_id", "status", "signal_date",
"intent_id", "root", "account", "legs", "contracts", "expiry", "exit_date", "last_close_date", "limit_price",
"max_price", "debit_usd", "max_debit_usd", "spot", "fallback_from", "entry_price", "fill_date"} plus "cost",
"exit_intent_id", "exit_signal_date", "exit_attempts", "last_close_miss" as it goes.

**Facts** (contract §8). NEW_TRADE: every contract key (`root`, `underlying_name`, `strategy_label`, `legs` with
tonight's bid/ask/mid, `expiry`, `contracts`, `limit_price`, `max_price`, `debit_usd`, `max_debit_usd`,
`max_value_usd`, `breakeven`, `spot`, `exit_date`, `stress_usd` = `max_debit_usd`, `stress_pct` (percent units),
`section_1256`, `settlement`, `base_rates`), plus `ticker`, `ticker_name`, `contracts_label`, `long_strike`,
`short_strike`, `width_usd`, `max_multiple`, `last_close_date`, `close_sessions_before`, `dte`, the signal
(`spx_close`, `spx_high`, `spx_drawdown`, `vix` and the rule constants), `fallback_from` (SPY only),
`american_root` (SPY only), `liquidity`, `admit_binding`. EXIT: `contracts`, `limit_price`, `max_price` (the stated
minimum), `credit_usd`, `min_credit_usd`, `entry_price`, `entry_date`, `mid_price`, `position_value`, `pnl_usd` /
`pnl_pct` at tonight's mid, `reason` "expiry_rule", `exit_date` (the session of this close), `planned_exit_date`,
`last_close_date`, `attempt`, `retry_date` (first attempt) or `retry_of` (the retry). Keys holding fractions carry no
`_pct` suffix (`spx_drawdown`, `drop_from_high`, `short_strike_ratio`).

**Forecast** (contract §9): one per NEW_TRADE, `event` "profit", resolved on exit: "The spread closes worth more
than its debit", p = `modules.M4.forecasts.p_profit` = **0.60** (§6). No new event, so `forecasts._exit_outcome` is
unchanged.

**Risk** (contract §7):
- `open_stress(positions, values, stress, cfg, *, spreads=None, pending=None)`: with either given, open spreads count
  at max(cost, mark × contracts × 100) in their module and cluster (M4 and XSP/SPX/SPY → us_equity; W8, W9 → oil),
  pending buys as if filled (spreads at the stated maximum; ETF orders at dollars × unit stress; sells, M2 and shadow
  orders left out). Extra keys: "by_cluster", "premium", "premium_by_cluster", "pending".
- `admit_premium(...)`: governor (unless exempt; M4 always is) → per-trade premium 3% → option premium 10% →
  factor premium 3% per cluster → cluster (us_equity 7%, others 6%) → total open stress 10%. Needs the extended
  book (ValueError otherwise: fail closed). ok is False under `min_premium_usd`. `cluster_overflow` is always 0.
- The premium caps are read from `risk.caps` (`per_trade_premium`, `option_premium`, `factor_premium`,
  `other_cluster`) when present, else `risk.PREMIUM_CAPS`, the design's values. I did not edit the `risk` block.

## 6. Evidence: base rates and the forecast

Base rates in the emails (track 21 §2.1, `research/code/21-duration/results/o2_mid_summary.csv`): the spread at 90 DTE
with a **next-day entry** (the mid of the next-open and next-close proxies), the frozen fill model, all 26 signals
1990–2026 in 12 episodes, per trade in % of the debit, priced on track 14's model surface:
- mean **+36%**, median +78%, **69%** winners, 4 of 26 (15%) lost the whole debit, worst −100%;
- 1990–2007 **+7%**, 2008–2026 +58%; episode mean +62% (t 4.1); deflated-Sharpe probability 0.67 (N 12), 0.19
  (N 840);
- edge over the same spread bought on random days: +23% of the debit, p 0.21 (`o2_placebo.csv`);
- 0.60 trades a year with one spread at a time (22 signals in 36.6 years, track 23 §3.5). Per track 21 §2.4 the
  per-trade figures use all 26 signals; no credit is taken for the one-at-a-time sample's +49% mean.
- Planning contribution (design §6, track 23 §3.2): **+0.04% of NAV a year at κ 0.25** (+0.13% at κ 0.5).

**Forecast p = 0.60** = p_base + κ × (p_hist − p_base) (track 17 R9) with p_base = 0.572, the win rate of a 90-DTE
call spread bought on any day 1990–2026 (`o2_placebo.csv`, `all_days_win`); p_hist = 0.692, O2's win rate above;
κ = 0.25 for 12 episodes (track 23 §3.2): 0.572 + 0.25 × 0.120 = 0.602.

## 7. Operations

- 22:17 ET daily run (after M1 and W10): M4 exit or entry; the twin's settlement and pending entry. Alerts: `data`
  (unconfirmed signal, missing/stale/disagreeing chain or close, unpriceable close), `liquidity` (both roots fail),
  `fill` (unfilled entry or close, an order still pending after its session, the last session passed), `shadow`
  (twin or probe errors).
- 10:17 ET options job: fills (hooks), the probe (armed days only), the twin's entry and exit.
- A missed session: an order whose 10:17 ET session passed with no fill decision is cancelled that evening. A missed
  close is re-issued as a fresh EXIT email for the next session; a missed entry is skipped and its forecasts voided.
- Owner setup: none beyond the options build's (Level 3 and index options on the taxable account).

## 8. Deviations and decisions (the design wins where they differ)

1. **Liquidity measured in market hours when available.** The brief says to decide XSP vs SPY with
   `liquidity_check` on tonight's chain; design §4 says liquidity is measured from the market-hours snapshot, and
   track 14 §6 found XSP's after-hours quotes stale and 3–10× wider (XSP also trades in Global Trading Hours from
   20:15 ET). So on "armed" days the 10:17 probe decides the root; otherwise tonight's chain does. Prices always come
   from tonight's chain (contract §2).
2. **Sizing at the stated maximum.** The contract counts per contract at the worst case (stated maximum × 100), so
   the 3% cap and the reserve hold even at the re-price.
3. **The cool-down is data-driven** (the research chain), started by every primary-data signal, confirmed or not.
4. **M4's admission counts pending buys**, so a W10 order queued the same evening goes first (first come, first
   served); M1 and W10's own admissions (pipeline) do not yet see M4 (integrator item 2).
5. **`admit_premium` also enforces "other clusters ≤ 6%"** (design §4) for W8/W9, with defaults in code because the
   `risk` block is not mine.
6. **The twin needs a confirmed signal** (fail closed on disagreeing data) but ignores M4's own admission and fill.
7. **Exit reason "expiry_rule"** (contract §8's list); M4 has no time stop of its own: the expiry ≤ entry + 90 days is
   the cap.

5. Check the three skip-marked tests run and pass after the merge: two `needs_options_build` tests (they skip while

## 10. Known limits

- **SPY fallback:** American-style, so the short call can be assigned early (typically before an ex-dividend date
  while in the money). Only a risk line in the email; no automatic handling.
- A nearly worthless spread whose legs have no bid cannot be priced for the close: an alert each evening, then the
  safety net settles it at expiry (at or near zero).
- The base rates and the forecast rest on a model-priced surface and 12 episodes; the paper phase, the twin and the
  10:17 snapshots measure them on real quotes.
- In this worktree (no options build) the provider has no `option_chain`, so a live signal fails closed with a
  `data` alert.
