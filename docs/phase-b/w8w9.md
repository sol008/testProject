## 2. The rules as built

### W8 — de-escalation confirmed (policy module, small)

At the 22:17 ET run, every night:

1. **The market map rolls.** For each trigger family (Polymarket "US announces end of Iranian blockade by <date>?"
   and "Strait of Hormuz traffic returns to normal by <date>?") the runner re-reads tonight's price of the market
   mapped last session, compares it with last session's stored snapshot, then re-maps by rule and stores tonight's
   price for tomorrow. Map rule: *the listed date nearest signal + 90 days, on or after the time stop of a trade
   entered tomorrow*. On 28 Sep 2026 that is "by Dec 31" in both families, track 17's market.
2. **(iii)** holds when a family's market is up ≥ 15 points since last session, or crossed 75% (a market that
   resolved Yes counts as 100%).
3. **(ii)** holds when the explicit front-safe Brent month (BZZ26 in late Sep 2026) or BNO closed ≥ 6% down on the
   day. One instrument crossing while the other rose fails closed (alert). BNO firing alone must pass the
   two-source close check.
4. When (ii) and (iii) hold, the candidate passes these gates in order; the first that fails logs a `shadow` record
   (book W8, with its stage and reason) and stops:
   one W8 trade at a time → the discretionary pause (drawdown ≥ 20%, a −2% day, a −4% week; design §4) → the trade
   budget → the scheduled-release ban → the invalidation market → the structure → the premium budget → **the LLM
   veto**.
5. **(i), the official announcement,** is evidenced by the veto: it may return PROCEED only with ≥ 2 distinct pages
   on the official-domain allow-list that its web search actually returned. Those URLs are logged.
6. The veto's request hash, sanitised response and verdict are written to the ledger (`signal`, check `veto`)
   before the email is rendered. Then NEW_TRADE: a `spread_limit` order in the taxable account, priced from
   tonight's chain with `fillmodel.order_prices` (limit mid + 0.3 × natural width, stated maximum mid + 0.5 × natural
   width, rounded to the cent upward).

**Trade.** A call debit spread on the first root that passes every rule, in the order XSP → SPY → DAL:
- the latest listed expiry 56–75 DTE from the entry session, then earlier ones;
- DAL legs expire strictly before DAL's next earnings date;
- strikes nearest spot × (1.015, 1.04) for XSP/SPY and × (1.01, 1.13) for DAL (track 17's Dec 780/800 SPY and
  Dec 85/95 DAL examples);
- the design §4 liquidity gate (`options.chain.liquidity_check`);
- at least one whole contract within 1% of NAV at the stated maximum.

Admission goes through `risk.admit_premium(module, root, premium, "oil", ...)`, not exempt from G(D).

**Exits** (checked every evening; the order goes out for the next session, after 10:00 ET), first match wins:
1. the expiry rule: ≤ 10 trading days to expiry (design §4);
2. invalidation: the mapped "US x Iran ceasefire continues through <first listed date ≥ the time stop>" below 40%,
   or resolved No. It is sticky, and the market is re-mapped by rule when it resolves or disappears;
3. take profit: the combo mid ≥ 80% of the width;
4. the time stop: the exit order goes out on the evening of session 20 and fills in session 21.

A close that the options job cannot fill is re-opened (`on_spread_cancel`) and re-issued that evening if still due.

**Never an oil short:** W8 refuses oil roots and anything but a bull call spread (config error → blocked + alert).

### W9 — escalation that removes barrels (paper)

- **Trigger:**
  - the owner's supply-loss record: ≥ 1 mb/d of additional exports physically offline, no restoration or bypass for
    ≥ 14 days, ≥ 2 sources on the allow-list (IEA, EIA, DOE, OPEC, company and official sites), `event_date` today
    or the session before;
  - the explicit front-safe Brent or WTI month up ≥ 5% on `event_date`. Sign disagreement fails closed.
- **Gates:** the same as W8, then the contango veto R3 (front-to-second WTI roll yield < −20% a year → no trade),
  then the structure, the premium budget (0.75% of NAV) and the veto.
- **Trade:** a USO call spread at spot × (1.10, 1.33), 56–75 DTE.
- **Exits:** expiry rule; invalidation (WTI closes below the pre-event close on the same explicit month, or the
  record gets `restored_on`); take profit at +100% of the premium; the 20-trading-day time stop.
- Each record gets one veto call at most. In live mode W9 stays paper: candidates go to the shadow ledger.

### What is logged

| Ledger record | When |
|---|---|
| `signal` check `trigger` | A mechanical leg fired, or both did (near misses included) |
| `signal` check `pm_remap` | A trigger family's map moved to another market |
| `signal` check `veto` | Every veto call, before the email |
| `signal` check `exit` | An exit is due |
| `signal` check `invalidation_remap` | The invalidation market was re-mapped |
| `shadow` book W8/W9 | A blocked candidate (event `blocked`, with stage and reason) or an entry the options job did not fill (`entry_not_filled`) |
| `correction` | Forecasts voided when an entry was not filled |
| `resolution` | A closed trade and its scored forecasts |

`state.modules.W8/W9` keeps the contract keys (`open_trade`, `history`) plus `events` (the last 60 candidate
outcomes), `pm_map` (W8's snapshots), `data_flags` (alert-once state for nightly feeds), `veto_model_sha256` and
`processed_events` (W9).

## 3. Interfaces

- **Runner hooks** (contract §7): `daily(run, checks)`, `on_spread_fill(run, fill, intent)`,
  `on_spread_cancel(run, intent, reason)`, `roots_needed(run)`, `options_job(run, chains)`.
- **Adapters are injected through the provider.** The runner reads `provider.prediction_markets`,
  `provider.macro_data` and `provider.option_chain(root)` (options build). A `LiveProvider` without the first two
  gets live ones attached on first use. With a provider that has none (e.g. the Phase A test providers) W8/W9 entries
  are skipped with a note.
- **The veto** is `run.services.veto` when set (tests), else `llm_veto.run_veto`. Signature:
  `(module, facts, cfg_veto) -> {"status": "proceed" | "veto" | "invalid" | "unavailable", ...}`; it never raises.
- **Facts:** NEW_TRADE and EXIT carry every key of contract §8 (`root`, `underlying_name`, `strategy_label`,
  `legs`, `expiry`, `contracts`, `limit_price`, `max_price`, `debit_usd`, `max_debit_usd`, `max_value_usd`,
  `breakeven`, `spot`, `exit_date`, `stress_usd`, `stress_pct`, `section_1256`, `settlement`, `base_rates`; EXIT:
  `credit_usd`, `min_credit_usd`, `entry_price`, `pnl_usd`, `pnl_pct`, `reason`), plus module keys used by
  `email_text/w8w9.py` (for example `brent_ret`, `pm_question`, `invalidation_question`, `veto_sources`).
- **Forecasts:** two per entry, resolved at the exit: "profit" (p 0.50 W8 / 0.40 W9, track 17 §5.2) and
  "time_stop" (0.60 / 0.45).

## 4. Owner setup

1. **Anthropic API key.** Create a key in the Claude Console. Add it as the repository secret `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
   Web search must be enabled for the organisation (Console → settings); with an organisation-level domain list,
   the allow-lists in `modules.W8.veto.allowed_domains` / `W9.veto.allowed_domains` must be inside it.
2. **The pinned model.** Add the repository secret `TRADEREC_VETO_MODEL` with a specific model identifier from the
   API's model list. Keep it unchanged: the ledger records its SHA-256 on every call, and a change raises a
   `veto` alert and re-pins. The model must support the tool version in `veto.web_search_tool`
   (`web_search_20260318`, current on 29 Sep 2026). For an older model, set `web_search_20250305` in the
   constitution (a rule change).
3. **Workflow environment (integrator).** The daily workflow must pass both secrets to the "Run the daily pipeline"
   step:
   ```yaml
   ANTHROPIC_API_KEY: ${{ secrets.ANTHROPIC_API_KEY }}
   TRADEREC_VETO_MODEL: ${{ secrets.TRADEREC_VETO_MODEL }}
   ```
   Without them every W8/W9 candidate fails closed into the shadow ledger, with a `veto` alert.
4. **W9's supply-loss record.** When official sources report barrels physically lost, commit
   `state/inputs/w9_supply_loss.json` the same day, or by the next session's 22:17 ET run:
   ```json
   {"events": [{"id": "yanbu-2026-10", "event_date": "2026-10-15", "mbd_offline": 1.8,
                "no_restoration_days": 21, "what": "Yanbu terminal and East-West pipeline damaged",
                "sources": ["https://www.iea.org/...", "https://www.aramco.com/..."],
                "restored_on": null}]}
   ```
   - Sources must be https pages on `modules.W9.supply.source_domains`.
   - Set `restored_on` when the barrels come back; an open trade then exits.
   - An unreadable file raises a `data` alert.
5. **Changing the prompt** means changing `llm_veto.PROMPTS`/`SYSTEM_PROMPT` and `veto.prompt_sha256` together (a
   rule change). A mismatch fails closed.

## 5. Cost estimate

The veto is called only for a candidate that passed every mechanical gate. The design expects about 0.4–2 W8/W9
events a year. Per call:

- up to 5 searches at $10 per 1,000 (≤ $0.05);
- about 10–40k input tokens (the prompt is ~1.2k; the search results dominate);
- about 1–4k output tokens.

| Price tier (per million tokens, input / output) | Typical call | Worst case (pause_turn continuations) |
|---|---|---|
| $1 / $5 | ≈ $0.05 | ≈ $0.20 |
| $3 / $15 | ≈ $0.15 | ≈ $0.60 |
| $10 / $50 | ≈ $0.45 | ≈ $2.00 |

That is under $5 a year even at the top tier, far below the design's "$5–15 a month" (which assumed LLM-written
emails; the emails are deterministic templates). A manual test call costs the same as one veto.

The market data is free:
- Polymarket: 3–4 requests a night;
- Kalshi: 3 requests per candidate;
- Nasdaq: 1 request per DAL candidate;
- Yahoo: 3–5 explicit-month and ETF series a night.

## 6. Choices where the design or track 17 left room, and deviations

1. **Condition (i) is the veto's citations.** An announcement market that resolved Yes is not a gate. Every trade
   needs a valid PROCEED anyway (fail closed), and resolution lags the headline by hours to days.
2. **Trigger-market rule.** The rule is "the listed date nearest signal + 90 days, on or after the time stop".
   A literal "first listed date ≥ signal + 90 days" goes blind from early October, because the Hormuz ladder ends
   on 31 Dec. The latest-dated market is rejected too: the blockade market for 31 Mar 2027 sits at 72%, a hair
   below "through 75%".
3. **"On the day"** means since the previous session's 22:17 ET snapshot of the same market, the window Brent's
   close-to-close move uses; a weekend announcement shows on Monday. Gamma's rolling 24-hour change is not used.
   With no snapshot from the previous session there is no trigger.
4. **Explicit months.** BZ uses month + 2 on days 1–15 and month + 3 after; CL uses `_contango_veto`'s + 1 / + 2.
   The design's "December contract" is this rule's output in late September.
5. **W9's barrel condition is an owner input,** validated mechanically. Track 17 R2 defines it "per IEA/EIA/company
   statements", and no free, real-time, machine-readable feed exists. A one-session grace follows R2's "classify
   within 24 hours".
6. **W9 exits beyond the design** follow track 17 §5.2 W9: take profit at +100%, and the WTI or restoration
   invalidation. Time stops are in trading days (track 17 R6).
7. **W8's invalidation is the design's market only.** Track 17's second condition (Brent retraces > 50% of its
   day-0 fall) is not implemented; the design text omits it.
8. **The release ban** covers FOMC, CPI and payrolls from Kalshi's calendars (track 14 §7.5 veto 1). The window is
   the entry session through five sessions later, per the red team's M5 wording ("entered ≤ 5 sessions before it").
   For entries from 30 Sep to 31 Dec 2026 this blocks 34 of 65 sessions (52%), roughly halving W8/W9's expected
   frequency; FOMC alone would block 12. The kinds are configurable, but narrowing them is a rule change. DAL
   earnings are handled by the expiry-before-earnings rule.
9. **Pauses apply.** W8/W9 are not among design §4's breaker exemptions (M1, M4, W10), so the 20% drawdown pause
   and the −2%/day and −4%/week breakers apply. The breaker thresholds are copied into the module blocks.
10. **Structure choices:**
    - strikes by moneyness from track 17's examples;
    - the latest expiry in the window first;
    - XSP before SPY before DAL (§3a.4 "XSP preferred"; Section 1256).
    A root or expiry that fails a rule (liquidity, one contract within the cap, earnings) falls through to the next.
11. **Liquidity is checked on tonight's (closing) chain** at decision time. Design §4 says "measured from the
    market-hours snapshot", but no hook runs before the 10:17 ET fill. `options.liquidity` is used when the options
    build defines it, else the module's own copy of the §4 numbers. No expected gain is passed (strict 10% round
    trip): USO and DAL will usually fail (track 17 R8), so W9 will mostly be shadow-logged.
12. **Prices are rounded to the cent**, buys up and sells down, so the fill model's price stays within the limit.
13. **The model is logged by SHA-256 only**, because the committed ledger must not carry an identifier. A model
    change alerts and re-pins rather than blocking. The prompt pin blocks.
14. **The web search is limited to the allow-list,** with `allowed_callers ["direct"]` (no dynamic filtering) so
    every retrieved URL is visible for the citation check. Structured outputs are not used, because the API
    documents them as incompatible with citations; the answer is parsed strictly instead.
15. **No new ledger record types:** the veto is a `signal`, a blocked candidate a `shadow` record.
16. **An unfilled entry voids its forecasts** (`correction`). The trade still counts against the annual budget,
    as Phase A counts at emission.
17. **An invalidation market already below 40% at entry blocks the entry;** the trade would exit the next evening.
18. **DAL's earnings date** comes from Nasdaq (named source, Zacks data). Yahoo can only move it earlier, by ≤ 7
    days; a larger disagreement or a missing Nasdaq date fails closed for DAL (SPY/XSP remain).

## 7. Known limits

- The veto can only see official sites. A reversal reported only by the press is caught by the ceasefire-market
  invalidation, not by the veto.
- Polymarket's displayed price is the midpoint or the last trade. A thin book can jump, which is one reason
  conditions (ii) and (i) are also required.
- Reaching Polymarket and Kalshi from GitHub Actions runners is untested; it works from this environment. A feed
  failure alerts once when it starts, then notes nightly.
- No historical option chains exist: the W8/W9 paper record starts now.
