# 《彼岸燈》 Shorelight — Story Bible

A Traditional-Chinese 仙俠 RPG in the spirit of 仙劍奇俠傳 (1995), set in a fantasy version of
**ancient Taiwan (Formosa)**. All text in-game is **Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)**, Taiwanese flavour
(閩南 words sprinkled lightly in NPC speech is welcome: 阿伯, 阿嬤, 囝仔, 歹勢, 甲飽未, 好佳哉, 夭壽).

Tone: warm, funny day-to-day banter → mystery → heartbreak → hope. Bittersweet like 仙劍, but with a
true ending earned by the player's kindness.

**Core theme — 記憶與遺忘 / 渡:** Is forgetting a mercy or a loss? Love that persists across
forgetting. "渡" = ferrying souls across the sea, crossing to the far shore (彼岸), crossing into
vision realms, and helping each other across.

## World lore

- **黑水溝 (the Black Tide):** the deadly current of the strait between 唐山 (mainland) and Formosa.
  Countless settlers drowned crossing ("六死三留一回頭"). In this world it is also the **sea of the
  dead**: every soul who dies becomes a small lantern-light that drifts out on the Black Tide.
- **彼岸 (the Far Shore)** lies beyond the Black Tide. There stands the **彼岸燈 (Shorelight)**, a
  great lamp lit by the ancient goddess **燈母 (the Lantern Mother)**. Its light guides drifting souls
  to the far shore, where they are washed of memory and reborn.
- **引魂人 (Soul Guides):** a secret order of humans who help lost souls on the water route (水路).
  They use 引魂槳 (soul-oars) and lanterns. Few remain.
- **中元普渡 / 放水燈:** on the 7th lunar month, people float 水燈 (water lanterns, incl. big
  house-shaped 水燈頭) into the sea to guide the water-drowned souls. The farther they float, the
  more blessing. (Based on the real 雞籠中元祭.)
- Every few generations, the Shorelight falters. When it does, its **燈靈 (lamp spirit)** is cast
  ashore in human form. If she does not return, the light dies, souls stop crossing, and the dead
  pile up as wraiths (水鬼 seeking 抓交替 substitutes multiply).
- **心燈 (Heart Lanterns):** when someone is truly helped / a soul is truly set free, a tiny
  memory-light is born — 心燈. This is the game's core moral mechanic and the key to the true ending.

### Psychedelic / visionary elements (treated as sacred, guided, meaningful — never as party drugs)
1. **七彩靈蕈 (Seven-Hued Spirit Mushrooms)** — glow in the 霧林 grove. Eating one grants
   **靈視 (Spirit Sight)**: colours breathe, edges ripple, the veil thins. Field ability: reveals
   hidden paths, ghosts, chests, true forms of 魔神仔 illusions. In battle the item grants the
   **幻境 (Trance)** status (crit/magic up, slight confusion risk). 糰子 eats too many and gives
   cosmic philosophy lectures (comic).
2. **夢藤湯 (Dream-Vine Brew) / 入夢禮** — an ayahuasca-like ceremony kept by **藤婆**, a centuries-old
   hermit herbalist of unknown origin who lives alone in the cloud forest below 玉山. The 夢藤 is a
   FICTIONAL spirit-vine that grows only there. (This rite belongs to no real people — real Indigenous
   cultures in the game are depicted accurately and are never given invented drug rituals.) Intention-
   setting, bitter brew, the purge (洗心), night fire, her chanting. Leads into **靈境 (the Vision
   Realm)**: a breathing jungle of living geometry, guided by a vast patterned **雲蛇 (cloud serpent)**
   and a **雲豹 (clouded leopard) spirit** — the extinct Formosan clouded leopard as a symbol of what
   is lost but not forgotten. Each companion faces their deepest wound.
3. **一息界 (The Realm of One Breath)** — a DMT-like breakthrough realm reached in a single breath:
   endlessly folding kaleidoscope temples, jewel light, impossible geometry. Inhabited by
   **萬象童子 (the Myriad-Form Children)** — playful, self-transforming jewelled entities who speak in
   colours, riddles and laughter. At the climax 星河 breaks through to reach the Shorelight's heart.

## Main characters

### 沈星河 (Shen Xinghe) — protagonist, 18
- Apprentice at 「陳記燈籠舖」 in **九份**. Found as a baby washed ashore at 八斗子 on 中元 night,
  lying inside a 水燈. Cheerful, lazy, quick-witted, kind; loves the teahouse storyteller's tales of
  劍仙 and 國姓爺. Talks big, panics small, is brave when it counts.
- Weapon: sword 「螢火」 (given by 陳伯). Element **火**. Balanced sword fighter.
- **Secret:** he drowned as a baby. The lamp spirit (白芷, in a previous falter) gave him a spark of
  her own flame. Ghosts hesitate before him; he faintly sees spirits; he has no soul-lantern of his own.
- Look: dark messy hair in a short topknot with loose strands, warm brown eyes, indigo short jacket over
  white inner shirt, rolled sleeves, dark trousers, straw sandals; red string bracelet; later a sword on back.

### 白芷 (Bai Zhi) — heroine, appears ~17
- Arrives unconscious on a great 水燈頭 drifting **in from** the sea (lanterns should only go out).
  Only memories: the song 「渡魂謠」 and a jade tag carved 「白芷」.
- Gentle, curious about everything human (first taste of 九份芋圓 is a big moment), speaks poetically
  and sometimes hilariously literally; slowly becomes more human, braver, funnier.
- Weapon: jade flute (玉笛). Element **水**. Healer / support / spirit-calming songs.
- **Secret:** she is the 燈靈 of the Shorelight. Every memory she regains makes her a little more
  translucent at the edges.
- Look: very long silver-white hair with a pale-blue sheen, loosely tied low with a red string and a
  small lantern-shaped hairpin; pale aqua-white layered dress (褙子 style) with sea-foam embroidery;
  soft gold eyes; a faint glow.

### 洛紅纓 (Luo Hongying) — 19
- Heir of **鹿港洛家**, a merchant & martial clan famed for 洛家槍. The clan was massacred three
  years ago on 中元 night. She hunts the masked swordsman 「鬼面」 blamed for it.
- Fiery, proud, blunt, honest, fiercely loyal; secretly loves cute things (糰子!); catastrophically
  bad cook (recurring gag). Calls 星河 「笨蛋」.
- Weapon: red-tasseled spear 「紅纓槍」. Element **金**. Physical DPS.
- Look: high ponytail of black hair with a long red ribbon, sharp eyes, crimson short robe with black
  leather vest and arm-guards, white trousers, boots; red tassel spear.

### 雲歸 (Yun Gui) — 24
- Taoist of **觀音山 太虛觀**. Sarcastic, lazy-eyed, drinks 米酒, reads forbidden texts on 「內景」
  (inner visions) and is an enthusiastic amateur mycologist (comic). Deeply loyal; searching for his
  missing master 清虛真人.
- Companion: **糰子 (Tuanzi)** — a chubby 石虎 (Formosan leopard-cat) spirit that talks, eats
  everything, panics, and, after eating spirit mushrooms, delivers absurd cosmic wisdom.
- Weapon: talismans + fan (符扇). Element **木** (thunder), but his talismans cover all five
  elements — the "weakness exploiter".
- Look: long black hair half-up with a wooden hairpin crown, narrow amused eyes, faded blue-grey Taoist
  robe with yin-yang sash, loose sleeves, gourd at hip, folding fan.

### Supporting
- **陳伯 / 陳渡 (Chen Du)** — grumpy, kind old lantern-maker; secretly a retired **引魂人**. Wields an
  引魂槳 (soul-oar). Raised 星河. Rows the party across the Black Tide in the finale.
- **夜玄 (Ye Xuan), 「玄冥法師」** — head of 府城 **長明宮 (Temple of Eternal Light)**, beloved for
  his 長明燈 that "ward off ghosts" (they actually trap souls). Former 引魂人 partner of 陳渡. Twenty
  years ago his 7-year-old daughter **夜念** drowned when their boat from 唐山 sank in the Black Tide.
  He caught her soul-lantern and kept it in a jar ever since, unable to let her be forgotten. He plans
  **逆潮 (Reversing the Tide)**: using the lamp spirit to turn the Black Tide back so all the dead
  return with their memories. Calm, gentle, tragic, and catastrophically wrong.
- **洛青鋒 (Luo Qingfeng)** — 紅纓's elder brother, 21, the masked 「鬼面」. Survived the massacre
  (done by wraiths when the Shorelight first faltered, not by him). 夜玄 saved and recruited him; he
  believes 逆潮 will bring their parents back. Uses 洛家劍. Redeems himself and dies shielding 紅纓.
- **清虛真人** — 雲歸's master; went to stop 夜玄, was sealed and possessed in the 燈母祠.
- **墨鴉 (Mo Ya)** — 長明宮 envoy in a black crow-feather cloak; first boss; comic-villain smugness.
- **藤婆** — fictional immortal hermit herbalist of the 玉山 cloud forest who keeps the 夢藤 rite. Wry, wise,
  smokes a long pipe, calls everyone 「孩子」, has outlived three dynasties and is unimpressed by all of them.
- **夜念** — 夜玄's daughter, a tired little ghost girl in the jar: 「爹，我好累……讓我走吧。」
- **燈母** — the Lantern Mother; appears only as a voice/vision of light.
- **萬象童子** — jewel-light entities of 一息界.
- Recurring townsfolk of 九份: **阿炳伯** (teahouse storyteller), **阿柑姨** (芋圓 shop),
  **小金** (kid with dog 阿黃), **阿土伯** (ghost of an old miner), **林大夫** (doctor).

## The journey: a full circle of Formosa in one year (一年一渡)
The game opens on 中元 (7th lunar month) and ends exactly one year later, on the last night of the next
ghost month, when the ghost gate closes (關鬼門). The party travels **clockwise-ish around the whole
island**: north → down the west coast → into the central mountains → south → up the east coast aboard
a spirit 王船 → out into the strait (澎湖) → the Black Tide → the Far Shore. Every region has its own
real culture, food, festival, folklore, and at least one 心燈. Seasons are tied to real customs:

### Chapter beats

**序章 夢** — dream bridge of lanterns over a black sea; tutorial vs 海霧魂; girl in white:
「星河……這一次，別再來找我了。」 → falls out of bed.

**1 九份燈火** — (as originally planned) morning errands; 舊金礦坑 tutorial dungeon (阿黃 the dog; 阿土伯's
pocket watch 心燈); 雞籠中元祭: 開龕門 at 老大公廟 is mentioned, the 主普壇 glows; the 放水燈頭 procession down
to 八斗子; lanterns turn blue and drift **back**; 白芷 washes in on a 水燈頭. Night: 水鬼 seeking 抓交替 climb
the 九份 stairs; 白芷 sings 渡魂謠; 墨鴉 of 長明宮 (boss); 水鬼王; 陳伯 reveals he is an 引魂人, is wounded,
sends them to 觀音山 太虛觀 to find 清虛真人. Sword 螢火, 引魂令.

**2 霧林天燈** — 魔神仔 looping forest; 七彩靈蕈 grove → 靈視; duel & join 洛紅纓; 虎姑婆 lair (rescue 平溪
children); 平溪: villagers tell the 天燈 origin; everyone releases 天燈 with wishes (白芷: 「我想知道我是誰。」
星河 secretly: 「希望她不要想起來就離開。」); 十分 waterfall. 心燈: woodcutter lost to 魔神仔, old couple's
son's letter.

**3 觀音艋舺** — 淡水 river port & 紅毛城 (side: ghosts of foreign soldiers who died far from home and can't
find their sea — 心燈); boat across to 觀音山; 雲歸 drunk on the 太虛觀 roof with 糰子; 照魂鏡 shows 白芷 as a
lamp-flame; 鬼面 night raid (紅纓 recognises 洛家 technique) → 雲歸 + 糰子 join. 艋舺: 龍山寺, 歌仔戲 troupe
(椅仔姑 possession), 阿蓮 & her drowned husband, ghost girl 小梅, two old ghosts still feuding from the
頂下郊拚 who learn to forgive (心燈 options). Clue: 清虛真人 went south along the coast road via 竹塹.

**4 竹塹風起** — The 九降風 has turned wrong: it howls with the voices of the drowned and米粉 racks rot.
竹塹 都城隍廟: the City God's ledger of the dead (生死簿) no longer balances — souls are missing; 范將軍 &
謝將軍 (七爺八爺) ask for help (deities treated with reverence — they speak through the temple keeper /
appear briefly in a vision). 客家庄 in the hills: 伙房 courtyards, 擂茶, 粄條; 紅纓 is challenged to a
山歌 duel (call-and-response minigame; she is hopeless, 白芷 is wonderful, 雲歸 cheats). A 義民 ghost who
died defending the village long ago wants his name remembered (心燈). The party is invited as guests to
the **Saisiyat 矮靈祭 at 向天湖**: they tie 芒草 (silvergrass) knots for protection as visitors do, keep
quiet, and only watch and listen. An elder explains the meaning: a wrong done long ago is remembered and
apologised for, every second year, so that memory becomes atonement rather than hatred. This is the
story's quiet answer to 夜玄 — memory should be honoured and released, not hoarded. 白芷 weeps without
knowing why. Dungeon **五指山風穴**: a cracked 長明燈 drawing in souls → boss 風魔. Clue: 清虛 went to 鹿港.

**5 鹿港舊夢** — 鹿港 天后宮 (the keeper tells how 媽祖 guides sailors across the Black Tide — 媽祖 is never
portrayed as a character; her presence is felt), 九曲巷, 摸乳巷 gag, 半邊井 generosity quest; **洛家古厝**
haunted mansion with flashbacks (青鋒 teaching 紅纓 the spear; she burnt his birthday noodles); boss 怨宴;
青鋒 unmasks, the truth, he leaves. 福伯's spirit (心燈).

**6 府城長明** — 元宵 in 府城: 赤崁樓, 大天后宮, 孔廟; a family's 做十六歲 ceremony at the 七娘媽 shrine
(白芷 asks whether someone who does not know her age can come of age — gentle scene). 鹽水蜂炮 night:
plague-ghosts amid the fireworks, the 關帝 procession's origin explained. 林投姐 (empathetic resolution,
心燈). **長明宮** stealth infiltration; 萬燈閣 of jarred souls; 夜玄's first appearance (calm, persuasive);
boss 冥燈護法; 夜玄 strikes 星河 down; 白芷 gives herself up to save him; 雲歸's 遁符 escape. 白芷 is taken
across the strait to 夜玄's true temple on 玄武嶼 (澎湖). 星河's flame begins to gutter.

**7 雲山入夢** — 陳伯: only the 燈母 can rekindle the flame; the way to her lies through dreams. The party
climbs into the mountains carrying the dying 星河 (he can still fight, with a slowly draining HP gimmick).
**阿里山**: a Tsou village (kuba men's house; hunting ethics; the belief that souls go to 塔山 — the elder
says the souls on 塔山 have grown restless, unable to move on, corroborating the crisis); 神木 and the sea
of clouds at dawn. **玉山 trail**: a Bunun hunter's family shelters them; at night they hear the 八部合音
prayer for the millet — harmonics rising like voices from heaven (the party listens in silence); the
Bunun flood legend: when the great flood came, people found refuge on 玉山 — "the mountain that does not
drown". **藤婆**'s cloud-forest hut → 入夢禮 → **靈境** dungeon (雲蛇 & 雲豹 guides; trials: 紅纓 vs phantom
青鋒, 雲歸 vs fear of failing his master, 星河 sees his drowning as a baby and the lantern-girl lending him her
light; glimpses of past cycles). He awakens **心火**. **日月潭**: a Thao elder shares the 白鹿 legend (the
ancestors followed a white deer and found the lake — a guide leading people home) and 杵音 by the shore;
Lalu island is the ancestors' place — the party respects it and does NOT go there. The **燈母祠** is a
fictional drowned shrine revealed beneath the lake mist by the 雲豹 spirit. Boss: possessed 清虛真人 → freed,
dies in peace, 雲歸 gains 「太虛神雷」. Murals of past cycles (心燈); 燈母's voice: "a lamp lit by one is
fragile; a lamp lit by a thousand never goes out."

**8 逆潮** — 3rd month, 媽祖's birthday pilgrimage passes through 北港. That night the first **逆潮** surges:
the Black Tide flows backwards and the drowned walk ashore along the west coast. Set piece: the party
fights beside the procession (八家將, the great 千里眼・順風耳 figures, 北管 drums and firecrackers) to protect
pilgrims; 鑽轎腳 blessing. Then 府城・安平 觀潮台: 青鋒 leads the "returned" — among them husks of his parents
who do not know him. He understands, shields 紅纓 from 夜玄's blast and dies, giving her the 洛家劍譜.
夜玄's voice over the sea: at the end of this ghost month, when the gate closes, the tide will turn forever.

**9 王船南行** — 陳伯: only a vessel the dead will carry can cross to 玄武嶼 and beyond — a 王船. South to
**打狗** (旗後 fishing village; 半屏山 legend side quest about greed: an immortal selling 湯圓, "one bowl
for a coin, two bowls free"), then **東港 東隆宮**: the party asks the 千歲爺 by **擲筊** — three
consecutive 聖筊 grant the 王船 on one condition: 「繞島巡境」 — it must circle all Formosa gathering light
and helping the tide-stricken before it may sail the Black Tide. **恆春** (old city gates, 落山風, an old
月琴 singer whose folk song carries the lost names of the drowned — 心燈) and **鵝鑾鼻燈塔** (a ghostly
foreign lighthouse keeper still tending the lamp; the lighthouse flame is taken aboard to light the 王船).
Boss in the south: 落山風 wind spirit / tide-sick sea beast.

**10 後山巡境** — The 王船 rides the 黑潮 (Kuroshio) up the east coast — the living, generous current that
brings the flying fish, counterpoint to the dead 黑水溝. **蘭嶼**: the Tao are wary of people trailing
ghost-trouble (death is taboo) and ask the party to stay by the shore; the party helps a family whose
拼板舟 was damaged by tide-sick creatures, learns the flying-fish season's rules, earns trust; an elder
gives the carved 船眼 eye-motif charm that guides boats home. **台東**: Puyuma 巴拉冠 young men in training /
an Amis village's 海祭 and age-grade elders — the party helps clear the coast of 溺魂 so the sea ritual can
proceed (心燈). **花蓮・太魯閣**: a Truku elder with 紋面 explains Gaya and the 彩虹橋 — only those who lived
truthfully cross to the ancestors. 星河, who has no soul-lantern, fears he can never cross; the elder:
crossing is earned by how you live, not by what you are. Gorge dungeon with marble cliffs (清水斷崖).
**宜蘭・頭城**: the last nights of the ghost month; the party joins the 搶孤, climbing the greased 孤棚 to
seize the 順風旗 — the fair wind for the final voyage. 龜山島 at dawn.

**11 黑水溝・彼岸** — The 王船 crosses to **澎湖**: 天后宮 (oldest 媽祖 temple), 石滬 stone weirs, basalt
columns; the tide-sick islands; 夜玄's true **長明宮 on 玄武嶼** (fictional). Then out onto the **黑水溝**: sea

## Cultural accuracy & respect guide (READ THIS — it is binding)
- **Real Indigenous peoples** that appear: 賽夏 Saisiyat (向天湖, 矮靈祭), 鄒 Tsou (阿里山), 布農 Bunun (玉山),
  邵 Thao (日月潭), 達悟 Tao (蘭嶼), 卑南 Puyuma & 阿美 Amis (台東/花蓮 coast), 太魯閣 Truku (太魯閣), 噶瑪蘭
  Kavalan (宜蘭, a mention/NPC). Depict them as individuals with dignity, humour, expertise and their own
  opinions — never exotic props, never "noble savages", never monsters or villains as a group, never
  "primitive". Use their own names for things where well known (kuba, Gaya, pasibutbut, tatala, palakuan).
- **Sacred ceremonies** (矮靈祭, 八部合音, 戰祭, 海祭, 祖靈籃, Lalu island) are only **witnessed or explained**
  by members of that people, never played as minigames, never entered uninvited, never altered by the
  party. Taboos are respected in-story (Tao death taboo; Saisiyat visitors tie 芒草; not going to Lalu).
- **No real Indigenous people is ever associated with psychedelics.** The 夢藤 rite belongs only to the
  fictional hermit 藤婆. 七彩靈蕈 belong to Han Taoist 靈芝/immortal-herb lore and 雲歸's eccentric study.
- **Han folk religion** (媽祖, 千歲爺/王爺, 城隍, 七爺八爺, 七娘媽, 關帝, 觀音, 土地公) is shown with reverence:
  deities are never bosses or comic relief; they act through temple keepers, 擲筊 answers, signs, a breeze,
  a vision. Temple practices (擲筊, 鑽轎腳, 遶境, 搶孤, 普渡, 做十六歲) should be described correctly.
- **Hakka** culture (竹塹 hills): 伙房, 藍衫, 擂茶, 粄條, 山歌 call-and-response, 義民 faith, 硬頸 spirit.
  Hakka NPC lines may include light Hakka flavour (e.g. 𠊎 = I, 恁 = so) but keep them readable.
- **History** is background flavour of the Qing era (no specific emperor, no named real officials). Avoid
  colonial-war politics. 紅毛城/赤崁樓/熱蘭遮 ruins are old relics of the Dutch/Spanish past.
- Folk monsters/ghosts used as enemies: 水鬼, 魔神仔, 虎姑婆, 椅仔姑 (as a lost spirit, not evil), 林投姐 (as a
  wronged spirit — resolve with empathy), 殭屍, 紙紮人, plague-ghosts (瘟鬼, never the 王爺 themselves), 落山風
  and 九降風 spirits gone wrong, drowned pirates' ghosts (澎湖), 鯤.
- Food & daily life to sprinkle in: 芋圓, 草仔粿, 米粉, 柿餅, 擂茶, 粄條, 牛舌餅, 蚵仔煎, 碗粿, 鹹粿, 虱目魚粥,
  飛魚乾, 小米酒 (offered by hosts, the party politely accepts a sip), 檳榔 (offered as hospitality),
  鳳梨酥, 仙草, 愛玉.
