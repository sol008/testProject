# Client visual references (binding)

The client supplied reference photos. The photos themselves are not stored in the repo. These notes describe what to take
from them. Use them as *inspiration for a stylised game character*, not as a portrait: keep the essence (features,
colouring, mood), then render in the game's style and 19th-century Qing-era Taiwan costume.

## 白芷 Bai Zhi (heroine): face & hair reference
- **Overall:** young East Asian woman, reads late-teens to early-20s. Calm, dreamy, slightly wistful, with a
  quiet, knowing half-smile. Her gaze often drifts upward and sideways, as if listening to something far away. That is
  ideal for the lamp-spirit, so use it as her default idle expression.
- **Hair:** very long, straight jet-black hair (soft natural wave toward the ends), reaching past the chest. Parted slightly
  off-centre, with long face-framing lengths, one side tucked behind the ear. Glossy, with a subtle blue-black sheen. Hair
  is mostly *worn down*; keep it long and flowing in most options. Use updos only as partial ones (half-up, ribbon).
- **Face:** soft oval face, gently tapered chin, smooth cheeks; fair skin with a warm-neutral undertone.
- **Eyes:** dark brown, almond-shaped, softly upturned outer corners, subtle double lid, calm and gentle. The look is
  mid-sized and elegant, not oversized anime eyes.
- **Brows:** natural, straight-to-softly-arched, medium thickness, dark.
- **Nose/mouth:** small refined nose. Naturally full lips in a closed, faint smile; soft rose-pink lip tone.
- **Signature mark:** a **small beauty mark (mole) on her right cheek, beside the nose** (viewer's left when she faces
  the camera), at upper-cheek level. Keep it in every design and in the 3D face texture.
- **Build/posture:** slender, graceful neck and shoulders, upright relaxed posture, elegant economy of movement.
- **Accessories cue:** a long dangling drop earring (dark bead / jade) and a thin dark cord bracelet on one wrist. This
  can merge with the story's red string (紅繩).

## Pets: two cat references
### 芋圓 Yuyuan (NEW pet, adopts 星河) ← cat reference A
- **Breed/coat:** sturdy, **chubby blue-grey tabby domestic shorthair** with a dense, plush, velvety coat. Silvery
  ticking catches the light. Soft darker tabby stripes on the forehead (classic "M"), cheeks and shoulders; paler grey-white
  muzzle and chin.
- **Head:** broad round head, full cheeks, **large upright ears** (pinkish inside, slightly flared).
- **Eyes:** round **olive / yellow-green eyes**, with a skeptical, deadpan, slightly grumpy "judging you" look.
- **Whiskers:** long, white, prominent.
- **Body:** big, heavy, loaf-like, with dignified bulk.
- **Personality:** silent, heavy, unimpressed by everything (including ghosts); has decided 星河 belongs to him.
  In battle he is an earth-element body-block assist. On the field he sits on pressure plates.
- (糰子 the 石虎 spirit is unchanged and separate; these two cats are NEW pets.)

### 麻糬 Mochi (NEW pet companion, travels with 白芷) ← cat reference B
- **Breed/coat:** large, very fluffy **long-haired dilute calico** (Siberian / Maine-coon-like floof).
  - Patches of soft blue-grey and cream/apricot on the head (asymmetric: grey over one eye and ear, a cream/apricot
    patch on the other side of the forehead) and on the back.
  - Mostly **white** chest, big ruff/mane, belly, legs and paws.
- **Face:** sweet, regal, slightly aloof. **Big chartreuse / yellow-green eyes**, **pink nose**, white muzzle, long
  white whiskers, ear tufts (lynx tips).
- **Body:** long-bodied and heavy under all that fur, with white "mitten" paws with toe tufts and a big plumed tail.
- **Personality:** gentle, serene, attaches to 白芷 at once; sees spirits; purrs when lanterns are near. She doesn't
  speak (contrast with chatty 糰子). She communicates by chirps and looks, and 白芷 seems to understand her.
- **Story hook:** a 九份 teahouse cat who follows 白芷 from chapter 1 (she senses the lamp-spirit's light). In battle:
  a support "assist" like 糰子 (healing purr / soothing aura, water-element), and she can find hidden spirit items on
  the field.
