## 01 洛家槍・承: Heir of the Luo Spear

**Direction:** faithful-historical · canon refit

**Idea:** 最「洛家」的一版：清代武家箭袖袍＋巴圖魯坎肩，紅黑白三色一眼可辨；馬尾高到像槍纓，倔強寫在剪影上。

The clan look, made real: a Qing martial-family arrow-sleeve robe under a black "baturu" vest — crimson, black and white read at any size, and the crown-high ponytail echoes the spear tassel.

**Palette:** 洛家朱紅 clan crimson `#b0242c` · 烏皮 black leather `#2a1d20` · 月白 moon-white `#e9e0d0` · 黃銅 brass `#c99a48` · 膚 skin `#f3d3bd` · 烏髮 hair `#21181f`

**Costume:** 高馬尾・紅緞帶 (crown ponytail, red silk ribbon); 巴圖魯坎肩 ("hero" vest, 一字襟 button row); 皮護腕 (laced leather bracers); 箭袖短袍 (arrow-sleeve robe, side slits); 薄底快靴 (soft-soled riding boots)

**Cultural basis:** Based on Qing 箭袖袍 (narrow "arrow" sleeves, side slits) and the 巴圖魯坎肩 one-line-button vest worn by martial men; the women's-cut waist, red ribbon and fitted trousers are a wuxia stylisation for spear work.

## 02 鹿港千金: Daughter of the Lukang Trading House

**Direction:** historical · merchant silk, road-worn

**Idea:** 「一府二鹿三艋舺」的大小姐：最好的絲綢、最講究的鑲滾，可是衣襬沾了三年的路塵——驕傲還在，靠山沒了。

The pampered heir of a great Lukang trading house: the finest silk and fussiest triple trims, but three years of road dust on the hem — the pride survives, the family behind it does not.

**Palette:** 胭脂紫紅 rouge plum silk `#8e2c48` · 玄緞 black satin trim `#1f1a1e` · 石青 stone-teal lining `#2e6a68` · 金線 gold thread `#d6ae58` · 象牙白 ivory `#efe6d6` · 膚 skin `#f6dcca`

**Costume:** 抹額 (velvet forehead band, jade bead); 步搖金簪 (swinging gold hairpins); 鑲滾大襟襖 (side-fastened jacket, triple trim); 寬袖反袖口 (wide sleeves, contrast cuffs); 繡花褲腳 (embroidered trouser hems)

**Cultural basis:** Draws on late-Qing Han women's 大襟襖 with wide 鑲滾 (multi-band) trims, embroidered trouser hems and the 抹額 forehead band seen in Lukang/Tainan wedding and festival portraits; unbound feet and the spear are the fantasy departure.

## 03 鏢局行腳: Escort on the Coast Road

**Direction:** historical · working traveller

**Idea:** 離家三年、跟著鏢車走遍西部海線的她：斗笠、綁腿、草鞋，槍桿上掛著全部家當——最接地氣的一版，笑起來露出小虎牙。

Three years riding with escort caravans down the west-coast road: rain hat, leg wraps, straw sandals, her whole life tied to the spear — the most down-to-earth take, with a crooked fang-tooth grin.

**Palette:** 褪茜紅 faded madder `#b55a3c` · 藍布 indigo cotton `#34405e` · 胚布 undyed cotton `#e6d8bd` · 竹篾 split bamboo `#d6b46e` · 牛皮 tan leather `#6b4630` · 曬膚 sun-tanned skin `#e2b48e`

**Costume:** 斗笠 (bamboo rain hat); 包袱 (travel bundle tied to the spear); 對襟短衫 (centre-buttoned jacket, rolled sleeves); 綁腿 (cotton leg wraps); 草鞋 (straw sandals)

**Cultural basis:** Built from 19th-c. Taiwanese labouring dress — 斗笠 bamboo hat, 對襟 cotton jacket, wide 大褲 with 綁腿 wraps and 草鞋 — plus the 鏢局 escort-agency tradition of wuxia; the women's braid and the spear-as-carrying-pole are stylised.

## 04 縞素誓: The Mourning Vow

**Direction:** historical-leaning · grief as armour

**Idea:** 三年孝期未滿，她不肯脫下麻衣：全身只剩槍纓一點紅。最冷、最安靜、最危險的一版——驕傲被悲傷磨成刀刃。

Her three years of mourning are not over and she will not take the hemp off: the spear tassel is the only colour left on her. The quietest, coldest, most dangerous take — pride ground down into an edge.

**Palette:** 生麻 unbleached hemp `#e4dccb` · 麻繩 hemp rope `#b9a27a` · 孝巾白 mourning white `#f4f1ea` · 鐵灰 iron grey `#3a3438` · 槍纓紅 tassel red (only colour) `#c41e2a` · 膚 pale skin `#f0dccd`

**Costume:** 孝巾 (mourning headcloth, long tails); 斷髮 (hair cut with her own blade); 不緝邊麻衫 (raw-edged hemp jacket); 麻絰腰繩 (hemp-rope mourning belt); 白布鞋 (white-covered shoes)

**Cultural basis:** Based on Han 披麻戴孝 mourning dress — unbleached hemp with deliberately unhemmed edges, the 麻絰 rope belt, white 孝巾 headcloth and white-covered shoes; wearing it into battle as a vow is the fantasy departure.

## 05 刀馬旦: The Opera Warrior-Maiden

**Direction:** bold fantasy · stage armour persona

**Idea:** 她說：「洛家的女兒，就要像戲台上的穆桂英。」把歌仔戲刀馬旦的「靠」改成能打的戰甲——雉翎與靠旗讓剪影在全場最搶眼。

"A daughter of the Luo should take the field like Mu Guiying on the stage." Taiwanese-opera martial heroine armour rebuilt to really fight in — plumes and back flags make the loudest silhouette in the cast.

**Palette:** 大紅 stage crimson `#c0182c` · 金片 gold sequin `#e0b040` · 點翠藍 kingfisher blue `#1f6fa0` · 翠綠 jade green `#3a9a7a` · 粉白 powder white `#f6f0e6` · 雉翎 pheasant plume `#c8a070`

**Costume:** 雉雞翎 (twin pheasant-tail plumes); 靠旗 (back flags of the stage general); 雲肩 (four-lobed cloud collar); 靠肚 (embroidered belly panel); 甲裙流蘇 (armour skirt with tassels)

**Cultural basis:** Inspired by 歌仔戲 / 京劇 刀馬旦 costume — the 女靠 stage armour with 靠旗 flags, 雲肩 cloud collar, 靠肚 belly panel and 雉雞翎 plumes on a 七星額子 crown; real stage armour is decorative, so its fighting construction is pure fantasy.

## 06 中元燈夜: Ghost-Month Lantern Hunter

**Direction:** fantasy · hooded night hunter

**Idea:** 每到七月她就出門獵鬼：斗篷兜帽、槍尖挑著一盞引魂燈，在中元夜裡找那個戴鬼面的人。暗色剪影＋一點燈火，是最有「仙劍」夜戰味的一版。

Every seventh month she goes hunting ghosts — hooded cloak, a soul-lantern hung from the spear-head, searching the Ghost-Month night for the masked man. A dark silhouette with one point of lantern light: the most "night-battle xianxia" take.

**Palette:** 夜藍 night indigo `#232a44` · 墨黑 ink black `#15121a` · 暗紅裡 blood-red lining `#9a1e2a` · 燈紙 lantern paper `#f0d9a0` · 符黃 talisman yellow `#e8c040` · 靈視眼 spirit-sight eyes `#e05a20`

**Costume:** 風帽斗篷 (hooded Qing cloak); 引魂燈 (paper soul-lantern on the spear); 鎮煞符 (talisman strips); 散髮 (loose hair spilling from the hood); 纏符快靴 (charm-bound boots)

**Cultural basis:** The cloak follows the Qing women's hooded 斗篷 (風帽) worn in winter; the paper lantern and yellow 符 talismans draw on 中元普渡 and Taoist exorcism practice. Glowing spirit-sight eyes and talisman-bound gear are fantasy.

## 07 綿甲・金: Studded Armour of Metal

**Direction:** grounded fantasy · Qing brigandine

**Idea:** 屬「金」的戰姬：清代綿甲的銅釘、護心鏡與護肩——以盾形與方塊堆疊出最「重」的剪影，擺出洛家槍起手式，像一面移動的城牆。

The Metal-element war-maiden: brass-studded Qing padded armour, heart-mirror and shoulder guards — stacked shield and block shapes give the heaviest silhouette, braced in the Luo-spear opening guard like a walking rampart.

**Palette:** 甲面朱 armour-face crimson `#a32430` · 鋼 steel `#8d97a3` · 鐵 dark iron `#3e444e` · 銅釘 brass studs `#d2a548` · 白襯 white lining `#efeae0` · 膚 skin `#ecc6a8`

**Costume:** 束髮冠 (steel hair-crown, top-knot); 護心鏡 (round steel heart-mirror); 護肩 (layered shoulder guards); 綿甲甲裙 (studded padded-armour skirt); 鐵護脛 (steel greaves)

**Cultural basis:** Based on the Qing 綿甲 (cloth-faced padded armour studded with brass rivets) with 護心鏡 and 護肩 as worn by Qing banner troops; fitting it to a 19-year-old woman, the steel crown and the slimmer cut are the fantasy departure.

## 08 海口躲藏: Harbour Disguise

**Direction:** historical · fishing-port everyday wear

**Idea:** 躲追兵時混進海口漁村的她：花頭巾、藍衫、圍裙、柴屐，長槍裹布假裝成釣竿——手上那條魚，是她「親手烤」的。最生活、最好笑、最暖的一版。

Hiding in a fishing village from her pursuers: floral kerchief, indigo jacket, apron and wooden clogs, the spear wrapped up as a fishing pole — and the fish in her hand is one she "cooked" herself. The warmest, funniest everyday take.

**Palette:** 藍衫 indigo jacket `#2c4a72` · 花頭巾 floral kerchief `#e07a8a` · 烏布裙 black apron `#3a3436` · 白褲 washed white `#f2ece0` · 柴屐 wooden clogs `#b98a56` · 曬膚 sun-kissed skin `#efc6a6`

**Costume:** 花頭巾 (floral kerchief over a low bun); 烤焦的魚 (her cooking (a recurring disaster)); 烏布圍裙 (black cotton apron); 捲褲管 (rolled wide trousers); 柴屐 (wooden clogs); 裹布藏槍 (spear wrapped as a fishing pole)

**Cultural basis:** Based on Hoklo coastal women's everyday dress — indigo 大襟衫, black cotton apron, wide rolled trousers, 柴屐 wooden clogs and a printed 頭巾 kerchief against the sun; the disguised spear and the comic charcoal fish are story inventions.

## 09 嘴硬心軟: All Bark, Soft Heart

**Direction:** stylised 6-head · tsundere, cute-lover

**Idea:** 把「偷偷喜歡可愛東西」穿在身上卻死不承認：雙丫髻上夾著石虎耳朵、槍上掛著石虎吊飾，嘴巴卻在說「才、才不是因為可愛！」6 頭身 Q 感，最適合宣傳與表情包。

Her secret love of cute things, worn openly while she flatly denies it: leopard-cat ear clips on twin buns and a leopard-cat plush on the spear — "I-it's NOT because it's cute!" A 6-head stylised take for key art and sticker sets.

**Palette:** 石榴紅 pomegranate red `#d02a3a` · 奶白 cream `#f6ead6` · 桃粉 peach pink `#f4a6b4` · 烏 ink `#2a1c22` · 金 gold `#e8b84a` · 石榴眼 garnet eyes `#b02a3a`

**Costume:** 雙丫髻・石虎耳夾 (twin buns, leopard-cat ear clips); 石虎吊飾 (leopard-cat plush (do not mention it)); 雲頭短褂 (cropped jacket, cloud-head corners); 燈籠褲 (gathered "lantern" trousers); 雲頭鞋 (cloud-toe shoes)

**Cultural basis:** Draws on Qing girls' 雙丫髻 twin buns, the short 褂 with 如意 cloud-head corners, gathered trousers and 雲頭鞋 cloud-toe shoes; the leopard-cat motifs reference Taiwan's 石虎 (and her companion 糰子). Proportions and accessories are stylised.

## 10 洛家家主: Head of House Luo

**Direction:** epilogue · matured, rebuilt clan

**Idea:** 結局後重建鹿港洛家的她：高髻、對襟長褂、腰間掛著哥哥碎裂的半張鬼面。最成熟、最沉穩的一版——驕傲終於長成了擔當。

After the ending, rebuilding House Luo in Lukang: high chignon, floor-length open coat, and half of her brother's shattered ghost mask at her hip. The most mature, composed take — pride finally grown into responsibility.

**Palette:** 玄色雲錦 black cloud brocade `#1d1a1f` · 洛家朱 clan crimson `#a8202c` · 銀白 silver-white `#e8e6e2` · 舊金 old gold `#b8975a` · 槍頭銀 spear silver `#7a8696` · 膚 skin `#efcfba`

**Costume:** 槍形銀簪・高髻 (high chignon, spear-shaped pin); 對襟長褂 (floor-length open coat, crimson lining); 兄長的半張鬼面 (half of her brother's ghost mask); 洛家槍徽 (clan crest buckle); 粉底靴 (white-soled formal boots)

**Cultural basis:** Based on late-Qing formal wear for wealthy Taiwanese households — 對襟長褂 over a 大襟 robe, 粉底靴 white-soled boots and a high 髻 with a single pin; a woman as clan head and the spear-shaped hairpin are the story's departure.
